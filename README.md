Entry for the make.fun competition **The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched**
(`u-plucky-sparrow-67-the-worst-app-idea-ever-pitched-a0396b23`).

`main.py` (Python stdlib only, deterministic) writes one pitch — a single
sentence of at most 240 characters pitching the worst, most useless app
imaginable — to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

## Entry

> SnoreSync livestreams your snoring to strangers who rate each snore out of ten, because your sleep deserves an audience and your neighbours deserve a monthly subscription.

(171 characters, one sentence.)
